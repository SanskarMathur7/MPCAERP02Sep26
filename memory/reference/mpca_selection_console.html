Minutes of selection meeting — draft







  
Selection protocol

  
    
    
  • Registration & eligibility
    The player must hold a current MPCA registration through an affiliated District, routed via the Division. Age verification (BCCI bone-age / TW3 protocol) must be on record for every age-group tournament, an NOC is required where the player was earlier registered with another State association, and anti-doping and anti-corruption education must be current.
    • 
    
  • Feeder tournaments
    Performance is drawn from the MPCA Inter-Divisional tournaments (Open / U-23 / U-18 / U-15), Inter-District within Division, the 'A'-grade Inter-Club competition and the Inter-School tournament, together with the player's BCCI domestic record. Scorers upload ball-by-ball data; the analytics cell normalises it by pitch, opposition and format.
    • 
    
  • Scouting & TRDO input
    Talent Resource Development Officers and Divisional coaches file written observations on technique, temperament and conditions-fit. They sit alongside the numbers — they do not replace them.
    • 
    
  • Probables pool & camp
    The committee, with the head coach, publishes the probables pool. Players report to the conditioning camp, where fitness testing (Yo-Yo, 2 km time trial, skinfolds) is recorded against the benchmark fixed by the Association.
    • 
    
  • Selection meeting
    Convened with notice; quorum required. Every member declares his or her conflicts of interest. A member related to, coaching, or otherwise interested in a player under consideration recuses from that name.
    • 
    
  • Name-by-name vote
    Each name is moved, discussed and voted. It carries on a simple majority of the members present — three of five, three of four, two of three. A name that carries goes on the shortlist; it enters the squad only if it is also clear of eligibility flags and a place remains in the 15. Where a red flag is waived, the ground must be recorded. The Chairman holds a casting vote in a tie. Dissent is recorded on request.
    • 
    
  • Balance check
    The squad of 15 is tested for role balance, left/right combinations, keeper cover, and the spin–pace mix for the expected conditions. Captain and vice-captain are appointed on the head coach's recommendation, recorded.
    • 
    
  • Ratification & announcement
    Minutes are signed by the Chairman and forwarded to the Hon. Secretary / CEO for ratification by the Apex Council. Squad, reserves and standbys are announced; call-up letters, contracts, match-fee terms and reporting instructions are issued.
    • 
    
  • Appeal & review
    An omitted player may seek feedback through the District unit; grievances go to the Ombudsman / Ethics Officer. The squad is reviewed after each match window and may be changed on form, fitness or disciplinary grounds.
