What this document covers: Sections 1–8 cover MPCA's own grant, subsidy, reimbursement, award and welfare schemes at the Divisional / District level. General GrantsAnnual grants & revenue-share to Divisional / District units
Annual Grants to Divisional / District Cricket Associations
Scheme 1-A · p.4-5
Grant per District (via Division)
₹1,10,000 / District / yearPassed on by the Division to the concerned District body.
Division-retained amount
₹30,000 / District / yearRetained by the Divisional Cricket Association to meet common expenses on activities conducted by the Division for the Districts under its aegis.
Periodicity
Yearly
Distribution Mechanism
Step 1: Districts submit required documents to Division. Step 2: Division compiles & forwards to MPCA. Step 3: MPCA scrutinises and releases grant to Division. Step 4: Division releases share to Districts.
Ceiling Adjustment
The annual grant ceiling is assessed against the District's spending pattern in the previous 2-3 years. If the pattern is less than 80% of the grant ceiling, the average amount (not the ceiling) is paid.
Important (w.e.f. 01.04.2026): The right of any District / Division to claim the annual grant shall stand automatically forfeited if the grant is not claimed within 12 months of the closure of the previous financial year.
Documents Required — From Division
Utilization Certificate (CA-certified) for the last grant received by the Division
Confirmation by Hon. Secretary regarding validity of the Division's registration under the Societies Registration Act
Auditor's Report & Audited Statement of Accounts for the immediately preceding financial year
Notice & Draft Minutes of the AGM in which the Auditor's Report / Accounts were passed (attested by Hon. Secretary)
List of members of the Divisional Association as on AGM date (attested)
Annual Budget of the Division for the corresponding year
Copy of documents (financials, member list etc.) submitted to the Registrar of Firms & Societies, with challan copy of payment
Bank statement depicting payment of the last annual grant to Districts
Documents Required — From Districts
Auditor's Report, Balance Sheet and Income & Expenditure or Receipts & Payments for the immediately preceding FY (preferably with CA-certified UC for the previous grant)
Notice & Draft Minutes of the AGM (attested by Hon. Secretary)
List of members of the District Association as on AGM date (attested)
Share to Divisions out of Net Revenue from ODI / T20
Scheme 1-B · p.6
Division's Share
4% of the surplus per DivisionOf net revenue (excess of income over expenses) from each International Match hosted by MPCA — as per audited figures. Effective from the ODI held in October 2015.
Applicability
Applicable for ODI & T20 matches. For Test Matches, applicability is on a case-to-case basis.
Periodicity
Per ODI / T20 match hosted by MPCA
Utilization Pattern (Mandatory)
70% of the surplus share must be utilised for one of: (i) Earmarked investment (FD) for District incentive schemes — annual interest income spent on the cause; (ii) Earmarked investment (FD) for rewards for junior players — annual interest spent on the cause; (iii) Any infrastructure development for a common cause.
30% of the surplus share may be used towards any other common expenditure of the Division.
Internal distribution within the 70% is at the Division's discretion, payable only after MPCA approval on a reasonable basis.
Documents Required
Copies of Fixed Deposit instruments for earmarked investments
Written norms for the schemes funded — District Incentive Scheme & Junior Player Rewards
CA-certified Utilization Certificate for surplus spent on infrastructure (common cause) & common expenditure for the previous paid surplus
Sequencing rule: Only after receipt of the above documents in chronological order shall the Division be entitled to the share for the next match or the next installment.

Incentive Scheme for Encouraging Outright Victories (Multi-Day Matches)
Scheme 4-D · p.24-25
Incentive paid to teams achieving outright victories in multi-day matches of both the BCCI circuit and the MPCA Inter-Divisional Tournaments. Scheme applicable only if the team reaches the knock-out / super-league stage of the respective tournament.

Monthly Benevolence Contribution (MBC)Welfare scheme for former First-Class Cricketers & BCCI / State Panel Umpires
Monthly Benevolence Contribution Scheme
Scheme 7-A · p.32-34 · payment effective 01.09.2025
Introduced in 2011 as a welfare scheme for former Ranji Trophy cricketers and former State Panel umpires from MP / Holkar — to provide financial assistance to those who contributed to the game when financial resources were minimal and who are not covered by the BCCI or any other state association's similar scheme. MPCA has created a corpus (earmarked investment) to fund the monthly payments.
Payment Slabs — PLAYERS
S. No. | Category | Amount |
1 | Cricketers who have crossed 75 years of age — irrespective of number of matches played | ₹15,000 / month |
2 | Cricketers who have played 15 – 24 matches | ₹10,000 / month |
3 | Cricketers who have played 5 – 14 matches | ₹7,500 / month |
4 | Cricketers who have played up to 4 matches | ₹5,000 / month |
Payment Slabs — UMPIRES
S. No. | Category | Amount |
1 | BCCI Panel umpires not covered under the BCCI's own scheme | ₹7,500 / month |
2 | State Panel umpires | ₹5,000 / month |
75-year increment: For Players S.No. 2–4 and Umpires S.No. 1–2 — when the beneficiary attains the age of 75 years, an increment of +₹5,000 is added to the last amount drawn under this scheme.
Core Conditions (since launch)
Applicable only to former first-class cricketers / state-panel or BCCI-panel umpires not covered by a similar scheme of BCCI or any other state association
A beneficiary can be entitled under only one category — either as a player or as an umpire, whichever is higher
Players: Must have featured in playing XI for Holkar / Madhya Pradesh; eligible only after announcement of retirement from first-class cricket and on crossing the eligible age (40+ years since Sep 2017)
Umpires: Must have crossed 55 years of age and announced retirement from umpiring
Scheme is not applicable posthumous — ceases on the life of the beneficiary
Late-enrolment cut-off: applications received on or after 01.01.2022 do not receive benefits for the period prior to enrolment
Revised Provisions w.e.f. 01.04.2025 — PLAYERS
Scheme is applicable only for players who have played up to 24 FC matches (regardless of whether they receive any gratis from BCCI or otherwise). Players who played more than 24 FC matches in career but not these many before the BCCI cut-off season are not entitled.
If BCCI in future extends gratis to players with fewer than 25 FC matches, the MPCA scheme will automatically be modified to remove names receiving BCCI gratis.
Any new player eligible w.e.f. 01.04.2025 (by matches criterion & other rules) shall be eligible for MBC after completing 50 years (original scheme norm).
Players not enrolled as on 01.04.2025 and who had the advantage of employment / gainful occupation / business shall be enrolled (if otherwise eligible) on completing age 60 — irrespective of later discontinuation of employment.
Players who have played international matches (any format, any number) or IPL matches (any number) after 01.04.2011 (launch of MBC), are not receiving BCCI gratis, and have played less than 25 FC matches — shall be eligible for MBC upon completion of 60 years.
A player who has retired from domestic circuit but is playing veterans / legends (or similar) tournaments — not considered until completely off the ground.
The scheme is not applicable to any fresh-enrolment player who has represented a team other than Holkar / Madhya Pradesh — irrespective of seasons or matches played.
Revised Provisions w.e.f. 01.04.2025 — UMPIRES
Any umpire (state panel or BCCI panel) inducted as State Panel after 01.04.2025 shall not be eligible for the scheme.
Any state-panel / BCCI umpire actively officiating prior to 01.04.2025 who has the advantage of employment / gainful occupation / business shall be enrolled upon completing age 60 — irrespective of later discontinuation.
Women's Cricketers: A Benevolence Scheme for former female cricketers of MP has been approved by the Managing Committee. Detailed norms are to be made accordingly.
The scheme is reviewed every year and can be discontinued if the situation requires, as per the recommendation of the Finance Committee.
